The Syllabus: Your Class Roadmap

A syllabus helps you understand how a class works, what your professor expects, and where to find the information you need throughout the semester.

1

Class Questions? Start with the Syllabus

As you get ready for your first semester, you may have questions like:

  • ?Will my classes be hard?
  • ?Will there be homework?
  • ?How many exams will there be?
  • ?What happens if I miss class?
  • ?How will my grade be calculated?
  • ?What books or materials will I need?
  • ?How do I contact my professor?
  • ?Where can I get help if I’m confused?

Those are great questions.

Every class is different, so there is not one answer that applies to every course. Once classes begin, however, many of these answers will be found in your syllabus. This page will help you use the syllabus to locate assignments, exams, grading information, due dates, professor expectations, and support resources.

What Is a Syllabus?

Think of it as the roadmap for your course.

⌘

A guide from your professor

A syllabus is a document from your professor that explains important information about a class. It shows you where the course is going and what you will need along the way.

Why the Syllabus Matters

In college, students are expected to use the syllabus throughout the semester, not just read it once at the beginning.

✓

Plan your time

Use due dates and course expectations to build a realistic weekly plan.

✓

Track major work

Keep assignments, exams, and projects from sneaking up on you.

✓

Understand expectations

Learn what your professor expects for attendance, participation, and coursework.

✓

Ask better questions

Check the syllabus first, then ask for clarification about anything that is still unclear.

✓

Find help early

Locate office hours, contact information, and support resources before you feel stuck.

✓

Avoid surprises

Know how grades, late work, attendance, and course policies are handled.

!

Important reminder

The syllabus is helpful, but it may not answer everything. After you read it, it is always okay to ask your professor for clarification.
