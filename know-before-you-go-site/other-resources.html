Other Support Resources

You might need help with more than academics. CSUDH has offices and staff who can help with health, well-being, advising, careers, accommodations, finances, and other challenges.

🧭
You do not have to figure everything out alone.

You may have questions about your health, well-being, major, career plans, accommodations, finances, food, housing, or another situation that is making it harder to focus on school.

Take a few minutes to become familiar with these resources now so you will know where to start when you need support.

Other Resources at CSUDH

Health & Well-Being

Connect with services that support physical, mental, and emotional health, including Student Health Services, Student Psychological Services, health education, and wellness programs.

Start here when you need: medical care, counseling, mental health support, wellness information, or help identifying the right health-related resource.

Visit Health & Well-Being

Student Success Centers

Each academic college has a Student Success Center that connects students with advising and other forms of support.

Start here when you need: academic advising, help selecting classes, information about your major, graduation planning, or university requirements.

Find your Student Success Center

Career Center

Explore career options, search for jobs and internships, prepare application materials, practice interviewing, and connect college experiences to future goals.

Start here when you need: career exploration, résumé or cover-letter help, interview preparation, internships, job-search assistance, or Handshake support.

Visit the Career Center

University Library

Access books, articles, databases, technology, study spaces, course reserves, research guides, and help from librarians.

Start here when you need: research help, reliable sources, course reserves, a computer or printer, a study space, or database assistance.

Explore the Library guide

Student disAbility Resource Center

The SdRC works with students with disabilities and medical conditions to provide access to reasonable accommodations and university programs.

Start here when you need: information about academic accommodations, assistive services, accessible materials, testing accommodations, or registration with the SdRC.

Visit the SdRC

Financial Aid & Scholarships

Get help understanding the financial aid process, required documents, financial aid offers, eligibility, grants, scholarships, loans, and work-study.

Start here when you need: FAFSA or California Dream Act help, questions about your offer, missing documents, scholarships, or eligibility.

Visit Financial Aid & Scholarships

Not sure where to start? You do not have to identify the perfect office before asking for help. Contact the resource that seems most closely connected to your situation, explain what you need, and ask whether they can help or connect you with the appropriate office.
