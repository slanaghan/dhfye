Know Before You Go: Where to Get Academic Help

College comes with questions, and knowing where to go for help can make a big difference. Explore these resources before the semester begins so you know what is available, where to find it, and what to expect.

Explore the moduleFind other support

You may even want to visit a resource, explore its website, or make an appointment before you urgently need help. Getting familiar with support early can make it easier to ask questions, stay on track, and get the most out of your semester.
