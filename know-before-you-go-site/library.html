Research, resources, and study spaces

University Library

The Library is more than a place to find books. It offers research help, databases, course materials, technology, and spaces for both quiet and collaborative study.

📚
Find information. Ask a librarian. Make space to study.

What Can You Find at the Library?

Books, Articles, and Databases

Use OneSearch and subject databases to find books, scholarly articles, streaming media, and other sources for your assignments. Current students can access many electronic resources from off campus using their CSUDH login.

Research Help from Librarians

Librarians can help you get started, narrow a topic, choose search terms, find credible sources, and use information responsibly. Help is available at different stages of the research process.

Study Spaces and Technology

The Library offers quiet study areas, collaborative spaces, group study rooms, computers, printing, and other technology to support your work.

Textbooks and Course Reserves

Some instructors place textbooks and other required materials on Course Reserves. Check early to see whether materials for your class are available.

Borrowing Beyond CSUDH

If CSUDH does not have an item you need, interlibrary loan and CSU+ may be able to obtain it for you at no cost.

Start with the University Library

Visit the Library website Explore research help

For research assistance, call (310) 243-3586 or email ask@csudh.libanswers.com. Check the website for current hours.

When Should You Ask a Librarian?

Ask for help when you are unsure how to begin, cannot find enough sources, are finding too much information, need a particular kind of source, or want help deciding whether a source is credible.

Tips for Making the Most of the Library

Start Research Early

Beginning early gives you time to try different searches, request materials, and ask for help before an assignment is due.

Bring the Assignment Prompt

The prompt helps a librarian understand the type, number, and level of sources you need.

Use Research Guides

Subject librarians create guides that recommend useful databases, sources, and search strategies for particular disciplines.

Save Source Information

Keep track of authors, titles, publication details, links, and database information as you research. This will make citations easier later.

Choose the Right Study Space

Use collaborative areas for group work and quieter floors or spaces when you need to concentrate.

Check Hours and Availability

Library hours, research help hours, and room availability can change. Check online before making a special trip.

Remember: You do not have to know exactly what you are looking for before asking a librarian. Helping you figure out where to begin is part of their job.
