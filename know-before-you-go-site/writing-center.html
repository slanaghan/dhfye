The CSUDH Writing Center supports writers from all majors and at every stage of the writing process. A Writing Associate can help you develop ideas, understand an assignment, organize your writing, use sources, revise a draft, and strengthen your skills as a writer.

How Can the Writing Center Help?

Support at Any Stage

  • Understand the assignment, brainstorm ideas, and create a plan
  • Develop a clear thesis and organize your ideas effectively
  • Use sources well and respond to instructor feedback
  • Revise your draft and identify patterns in grammar or sentence structure

Writing Support for Any Course

The Writing Center is not only for English classes. You can bring essays, research papers, lab reports, presentations, personal statements, and other academic projects from any course or discipline.

Different Ways to Meet

  • In-person tutoring: Meet at the Writing Center.
  • Online tutoring: Meet in a live online appointment.
  • eTutoring: Upload a draft and receive written feedback.

In-person walk-ins may also be available, but appointments are recommended.

Remember: You can make an appointment when you have an idea, a question, an assignment prompt, or a blank page.

How to Make an Appointment

Create a WC Online Account

The Writing Center uses WC Online to schedule appointments. The first time you visit, create an account using your ToroMail email address. Allow notifications so you receive confirmations and reminders.

Choose Your Appointment

Review the available dates and times, choose the format that works for you, and provide information about the assignment and what you would like to work on.

Tips for Making the Most of a Writing Center Appointment

Visit Before the Assignment Is Due

Schedule early enough to use what you learn and revise your work. You can return as your writing develops.

Bring the Assignment Instructions

Bring or upload the prompt, rubric, instructor feedback, and any other materials that clarify what you are being asked to do.

Bring Whatever You Have

You can bring notes, an outline, a partial draft, a complete draft, or simply your ideas. You do not need a polished document.

Participate in the Conversation

An appointment is collaborative. Be ready to talk about your goals, explain your ideas, and make decisions about your writing.

Do Not Expect a Proofreading Service

Writing Associates will not simply correct every error or rewrite your paper. They help you identify patterns and develop reusable strategies.

Revise After Your Appointment

Review your notes and use the ideas or strategies you discussed. Think about how you can apply them to future assignments.

Remember: The goal is not just to improve one paper. It is to build strategies, confidence, and skills you can use whenever you write.
