Instructor Office Hours

Office hours are dedicated time to ask questions, review feedback, discuss assignments, and build a connection with your instructor.

What are office hours?TipsRemember

What Are Office Hours?

Dedicated Time to Meet with Your Instructor

Office hours are times your instructors set aside to meet with students outside of class. You can usually find the days, times, location, and information about how to attend in your syllabus or Canvas course.

More Than a Place to Ask Questions

Office hours are not only for students who are confused or falling behind. You can also use them to:

  • Introduce yourself and get to know your instructor
  • Ask for clarification about something discussed in class
  • Review feedback on an assignment or exam
  • Check whether you are approaching an assignment correctly
  • Talk about study strategies or how to improve
  • Ask about the subject, major, careers, or future classes
  • Discuss a concern affecting your participation or progress
Remember: You do not need to have a major problem or a perfectly prepared question before attending office hours.

Tips for Making the Most of Office Hours

Go Early in the Semester

You do not have to wait until you are struggling. Consider attending early to introduce yourself, ask a question, or learn more about the course.

Check the Instructions Before You Go

Some office hours are held in person, while others may take place on Zoom or require an appointment. Check your syllabus or Canvas course for details.

Bring Your Materials

Bring anything that will help explain your question:

  • Your notes
  • The assignment instructions
  • A problem you attempted
  • A draft of your work
  • Feedback you received
  • Your textbook or course materials

Ask Specific Questions

Instead of saying, “I don’t understand anything,” try:

  • “Can you help me understand where I went wrong?”
  • “Am I interpreting these instructions correctly?”
  • “What should I focus on as I study for the exam?”
  • “Can we talk about the feedback on my paper?”
  • “What could I do differently next time?”

Take Notes

Write down important advice, explanations, or next steps so that you can refer to them later.

Follow Up

After the meeting, use the advice you received. Return to office hours if you still have questions or want feedback on your progress.

Remember: Going to office hours is a normal part of being a college student. You are not bothering your instructor. Office hours are time specifically set aside to support you.
