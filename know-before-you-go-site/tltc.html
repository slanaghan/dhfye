Free campus tutoring

Toro Learning and Testing Center

The TLTC provides free tutoring and academic support to currently enrolled CSUDH students. You do not have to wait until you are struggling to use it.

Tutoring servicesContactTips
Tutoring can help you strengthen your understanding, practice course material, develop study strategies, and prepare for assignments and exams.

What Tutoring Services Does the TLTC Offer?

Drop-In Group Tutoring

The TLTC offers in-person group tutoring for a variety of courses and subjects. No appointment is required. Visit the Learning Center in North Library 1575, check in at the front desk, and you will be connected with an available tutor on a first-come, first-served basis.

Because tutoring is offered in a group setting, you can ask questions, practice, and learn from the questions and approaches of your classmates.

Independent and Group Study Support

You can visit the Learning Center to study independently or with classmates. Check in at the front desk to be assigned to a table. If you need help while studying, ask whether a tutor is available.

After-Hours Tutoring Through NetTutor

When the Learning Center is closed, you may be able to access tutoring through NetTutor. NetTutor is available through Canvas and offers after-hours tutoring in a range of subjects.

Tips for Making the Most of a Tutoring Session

Go Before You Fall Behind

You do not have to wait until you are completely confused. Visiting tutoring early can help you address small questions before they become larger problems.

Bring Your Course Materials

  • Your class notes
  • The assignment instructions
  • Your textbook or course materials
  • Practice problems
  • A study guide
  • A problem you have already attempted

Try the Work First

Make an attempt before asking for help. Your work will help the tutor see what you understand and where you may be getting stuck.

Explain Your Thinking

Talk through what you tried. Explaining your process helps the tutor understand how you are approaching the material and provide more useful guidance.

Participate Actively

A tutor may ask you questions, have you explain a concept, or ask you to try another problem. Tutoring is most useful when you participate.

Take Notes

Write down explanations, strategies, examples, and next steps so you can continue working after the session.

Practice After the Session

Try another problem or apply the strategy on your own. This will help you see what you now understand and what questions remain.

Return Regularly

Regular visits can help you stay current with the course, prepare for exams, and continue developing effective study habits.

Remember: Tutors can help you understand concepts and develop strategies, but they will not complete assignments or do the work for you.
