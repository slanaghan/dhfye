Plan Your Week

A simple weekly plan can help you balance classes, responsibilities, connections, and time for yourself.

You Do Not Have to Have It All Figured Out

You do not have to be perfect at managing your time before college starts. Most students learn by doing. Start with a simple plan, try it for a week, and adjust it as you learn what works best for you.

What to Include in Your Weekly Plan

  • Class time
  • Work or family responsibilities
  • Study time
  • Commuting time
  • Meals
  • Time to check Canvas and email
  • Time to connect with people or get involved
  • Time to relax and have fun
Remember: Your first plan does not need to be perfect. A useful schedule is one that you can update as your needs change.
