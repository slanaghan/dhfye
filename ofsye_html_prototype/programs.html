First-year experience

Dominguez Hills First-Year Experience

DHFYE brings students together in supportive learning communities that connect courses, faculty, classmates, and campus resources.

  • Build relationships with classmates and faculty.
  • Develop academic strategies.
  • Learn about campus resources.
  • Reflect on your goals and educational journey.

Connected courses

First-Year Interest Groups

FIGs are connected courses organized around a theme, academic interest, or student population.

  • Take classes with some of the same students.
  • Make connections across subjects.
  • Build a supportive academic community.
Explore FIGs
Summer opportunity

Summer FIGs

Start your CSUDH experience early by earning credit, meeting other students, and becoming familiar with college expectations.

  • Take connected summer courses.
  • Participate in workshops.
  • Build confidence before fall.
Learn about Summer FIGs
Incoming students

Toro Launch

Toro Launch offers student-friendly workshops and resources that help incoming students prepare for their first semester.

  • Learn what to expect in college.
  • Understand a syllabus and manage your time.
  • Find academic and campus support.
  • Begin building career-readiness skills.
View Toro Launch resources
Second-year experience

Toro Advance

Toro Advance helps students reflect on their first year, explore majors and careers, get involved, and plan meaningful next steps.

  • Identify skills developed through your experiences.
  • Explore majors and careers.
  • Build relationships and networks.
  • Plan your next academic and professional steps.
