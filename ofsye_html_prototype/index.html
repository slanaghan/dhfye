Your first two years at CSUDH

Start strong. Find your people. Build what comes next.

Your first two years are a time to learn how college works, connect with people and resources, explore your interests, and build confidence. OFSYE helps you find the programs, support, and opportunities that can make that journey easier.

Your journey

Support throughout your first two years

OFSYE connects the pieces so students can move from getting ready to choosing meaningful next steps.

Before classes

Complete important steps, learn campus systems, explore summer programs, and ask questions early.

First semester

Build routines, meet people, learn to use support, and become familiar with college expectations.

Rest of year one

Explore your interests, get involved, strengthen academic habits, and reflect on what you are learning.

Second year

Clarify your goals, explore careers, seek meaningful experiences, and prepare for upper-division study.
