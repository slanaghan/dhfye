A guide for students

Your first two years at CSUDH

College is not only about completing classes. It is also about learning what works for you, building relationships, exploring possibilities, and developing the skills you will use throughout college and beyond.

You do not need to complete every step at once. Choose one or two actions that make sense for where you are now.

Before your first semester

Before classes begin, you can take steps that will make it easier to navigate your first weeks.

  • Complete orientation and enrollment requirements.
  • Review your class schedule.
  • Learn how to use Canvas and your CSUDH email.
  • Identify your academic advising office.
  • Explore financial aid and payment information.
  • Learn where to find tutoring and academic support.
  • Plan how you will travel to and around campus.
  • Explore summer courses and transition programs.
  • Attend a Toro Launch workshop.

Your first semester

Your first semester is a time to learn how your courses work, meet people, and practice asking for help.

  • Read the syllabus for each course.
  • Attend class and participate regularly.
  • Check Canvas and your CSUDH email.
  • Introduce yourself to your professors.
  • Learn how to use office hours.
  • Create a weekly schedule.
  • Connect with classmates.
  • Visit tutoring before you feel behind.
  • Join one activity, organization, or campus event.
  • Meet with an academic advisor.

The rest of your first year

As you become more familiar with college, begin exploring what you may want your CSUDH experience to include.

  • Learn more about your major or explore another option.
  • Attend a Career Center event.
  • Talk with a faculty member about your interests.
  • Join a student organization.
  • Look for volunteer, leadership, research, or campus employment opportunities.
  • Reflect on the skills you are already developing.
  • Make a plan for your second year.

Your second year

Your second year is an important time to reflect on your progress and become more intentional about what comes next.

  • Review your academic plan.
  • Confirm or continue exploring your major.
  • Learn about careers connected to your interests.
  • Build relationships with faculty and staff.
  • Explore internships, research, service, leadership, or campus employment.
  • Strengthen your résumé and career story.
  • Prepare for upper-division coursework.
  • Identify one meaningful goal for the year.
